Saanp Seerhi Party – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Saanp Seerhi Party ("the Game", package com.saanpseedhi.party) handles information. The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

Information stored on your device

The Game has no sign-up and does not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The player name and avatar you choose, the names typed in for pass-and-play, your coins, gems, statistics and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Online rooms with friends

When you create or join an online room with a room code, your chosen player name, avatar, moves and chat messages (text, quick phrases and emojis) are sent to the other players in that room only. Players are connected directly to each other (peer to peer) where possible. To set up these direct connections the Game uses the public PeerJS signalling service and standard connection-helper (STUN/TURN) servers (Google STUN and the Open Relay project); as with any direct connection, the other players' devices and these services can see your device's IP address.

If a direct connection cannot be made, game messages pass through the Game's own relay server. For this the Game silently creates an anonymous guest account on that server: a random ID and session token with no name, email or other personal details. The server keeps that guest record (ID, session token and when it was last used) so it can recognise returning devices, and forgets it after 90 days without use. Game messages, including chat, only pass through the relay server in transit to the other players in your room; they are not stored. The server keeps short technical logs (such as connection times and IP addresses) for security and abuse prevention, and counters such as the number of open rooms.

Voice chat and the microphone

Voice chat is optional and off by default. The Game asks for microphone permission only when you switch your microphone on in an online room, and it uses the microphone only while voice chat is on. Voice audio goes directly between the players' devices in your room (peer to peer, or through a TURN connection helper when a direct path is blocked). It is not sent to our relay server and is never recorded or stored by the Game. You can switch your microphone off at any time, and mute any other player.

Chat safety

Chat is only available inside a room with players you invited with a room code. You can mute any other player from the in-game player list (this hides their text chat and emojis and silences their voice on your device). The Game masks common offensive words in text chat. If you see abuse, leave the room and contact us at the address below.

Advertising

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), an ad mediation service, and its ad network partners. To serve, measure and limit the frequency of ads and to prevent fraud, these providers may automatically collect:

  • the device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and app set ID,
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it,
  • device and operating-system information (model, OS version, language, screen size, network type) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information about the ads,
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed or clicked).

This data is processed by the ad providers under their own privacy policies, including the Unity / ironSource privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Full-screen ads are shown only between matches (after a finished match, at most every second match and not more than once every 90 seconds), never during a match. Watching a video ad for extra gems is always optional.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the players in your room and the services described above, no data is shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the Game deletes everything stored on your device. The anonymous guest record on the relay server is deleted automatically after 90 days without use; you can also ask us to delete it sooner by email.
